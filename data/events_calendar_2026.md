# GDG On Campus USAR - Annual Events Calendar 2026

## 1. Overview & Calendar Schedule
GDG On Campus USAR hosts several major technical events throughout the 2026 academic year. All registered students of USAR are eligible to attend general technical sessions.

- **HackUSAR 2026 (Annual Hackathon)**: Scheduled for November 14–15, 2026. Venue: Main Campus USAR Auditorium & IoT Labs. A 24-hour hackathon focused on AI and Sustainable Cities. Teams must consist of 2 to 4 members.
- **AI Bootcamp & Vision Workshop**: Scheduled for September 18, 2026. Venue: Computer Center Lab 201. Hands-on computer vision and multimodal agent development.
- **Cloud Study Jam**: Scheduled for October 5, 2026. Venue: Virtual on Google Meet. Hands-on labs covering Google Cloud Platform fundamentals and Vertex AI credits.

## 2. Event Registration & Ticketing
Registration for all GDG USAR events opens two weeks before the event date via the official GDG community chapter portal. Tickets are free but non-transferable.

## 3. Workshop Certificate Note & Discrepancies
An informal club bulletin mentioned that workshop certificates are automatically issued to all attendees who scan the QR code at the door. However, students must follow the official GDG-USAR Student Handbook policy, which clarifies that workshop attendance does NOT automatically grant a certificate unless explicitly stated and criteria are met.
